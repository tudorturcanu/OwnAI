# App Store ASO Recommendations for Own AI

Assumption: the shipped brand is `Own Ai` today, but the App Store listing should standardize it to `Own AI` for readability and search consistency.

## Recommended Metadata

### App Name
`Own AI: Private Chat`

Why:
- Keeps the brand first.
- Uses high-intent discovery terms already supported by the product.
- Leaves room for future iteration without hitting Apple's 30-character limit.

### Subtitle
`Voice, PDFs & Local Models`

Why:
- Adds non-duplicative feature terms.
- Matches real product capabilities in the app today.
- Balances discoverability with a readable value proposition.

### Promotional Text
`Chat privately with on-device AI, switch to Apple Intelligence when available, ask questions about PDFs, and talk hands-free with voice mode.`

### Keywords
`offline,ondevice,assistant,document,reader,summarizer,speech,appleintelligence,llm,rag`

Why this set:
- Focuses on intent terms not already covered by the name and subtitle.
- Avoids competitor names and irrelevant broad terms.
- Leans into the app's strongest differentiation: privacy, local inference, and document chat.

## Description

`Own AI is a private AI assistant for iPhone that helps you chat, speak, and ask questions about documents using on-device models or Apple Intelligence when you choose it.

Built for people who want more control over their AI experience, Own AI keeps local model conversations on your device while giving you the option to use Apple Intelligence on supported devices.

What you can do with Own AI:

- Chat with AI using local models downloaded to your device
- Use Apple Intelligence when available and enabled
- Talk naturally with voice input and conversation mode
- Import PDFs, DOCX, TXT, RTF, and code files, then ask questions about them
- Summarize documents and retrieve relevant context inside each chat
- Pick the model that fits your device and needs
- Customize your assistant with different AI personalities
- Keep conversation history stored locally on your device

Own AI is designed for privacy-conscious users, students, researchers, founders, and anyone who wants a more personal AI workflow without depending entirely on the cloud.

Download Own AI to get a faster, more private way to chat, read documents, and work with AI on your iPhone.`

## Positioning Notes

- Lead with `private AI` and `on-device` in screenshots and first-line copy.
- Treat `document chat` and `voice` as the next two conversion hooks.
- Mention Apple Intelligence as a supported option, not the primary promise.
- Keep the product name consistently written as `Own AI` across the App Store, website, and in-app marketing.

Visual guidance:

- Show a real PDF import flow by screenshot 3.

- Keep each screenshot focused on one job to improve scanability in search and browse.

## Custom Product Page Ideas

1. Privacy page
   Focus keywords and screenshots around private AI, on-device AI, offline assistant.
2. Document page
   Focus on PDF chat, document Q&A, summarizer, and research workflows.
3. Voice page
   Focus on voice assistant, speech input, and hands-free chat.

Apple allows custom product pages to carry their own keywords, so these are worth testing after the default page is stable.
